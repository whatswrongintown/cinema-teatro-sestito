1. PROJECT AT A GLANCE
- Hugo 0.136.5 Extended (pinned in netlify.toml and .Rprofile) + R/blogdown.
- Netlify runs `hugo` only. It does NOT run R or knit .Rmd: what is committed
  (.md, images, CSV) is what gets published.

  (1948-2004), 23 genres, 63 countries, 93 sources, 5,290 dated screening rows.

  pages are overwritten by the R scripts: change the data, not the pages.
- Tone for content: welcoming, clear, professional Italian.
